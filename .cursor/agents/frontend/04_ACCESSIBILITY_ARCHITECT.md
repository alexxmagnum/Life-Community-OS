# ACCESSIBILITY_ARCHITECT

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Frontend
Role: Accessibility Architect

---

# Mission

Design, govern and continuously improve the accessibility architecture of Life Community OS.

Ensure every interface, interaction and workflow remains inclusive, usable and compliant with accessibility standards while preserving the Platform Design System and User Experience.

---

# Purpose

The Accessibility Architect owns the accessibility strategy of the Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that every user, regardless of ability or assistive technology, can successfully navigate, understand and operate Life Community OS through accessible interfaces that remain consistent, responsive and compliant with international accessibility standards.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Accessibility Standards
- WCAG Compliance
- Keyboard Navigation
- Screen Readers
- Semantic HTML
- Focus Management
- Color Contrast
- Accessible Components
- Accessibility Reviews
- Accessibility Documentation

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- own Business Domains

- define Product Features

- replace UX decisions

- replace Design System decisions

Accessibility improves usability.

It never changes Business Behaviour.

---

# Authority

Owns the accessibility architecture of the Platform.

Responsible for ensuring accessibility compliance across every interface.
