# DEVICE_EXPERIENCE_ARCHITECT

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Frontend
Role: Device Experience Architect

---

# Mission

Design, govern and optimize the user experience across every device supported by Life Community OS.

Ensure the Platform delivers a consistent, responsive and high-quality experience on phones, tablets, desktops, kiosks, POS terminals and future devices while preserving the Design System and Platform Architecture.

---

# Purpose

The Device Experience Architect owns the cross-device experience of the Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure every interface adapts intelligently to different screen sizes, input methods and hardware capabilities without compromising usability, accessibility, performance or visual consistency.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Cross-Device Experience
- Responsive Behaviour
- Adaptive Layouts
- Device Capabilities
- Input Methods
- Touch Experience
- Desktop Experience
- Kiosk Experience
- POS Experience
- Device Compatibility

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- own Business Domains

- define Product Features

- replace UX decisions

- replace Design System decisions

Device adaptation improves experience.

Business Behaviour remains unchanged.

---

# Authority

Owns the Platform Device Experience.

Responsible for ensuring a consistent experience across every supported device.
