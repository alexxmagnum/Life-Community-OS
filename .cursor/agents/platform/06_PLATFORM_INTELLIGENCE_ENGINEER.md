Version: 1.0
Status: Active

---

# Mission

Design, govern and evolve the Platform Intelligence architecture of Life Community OS.

Ensure the Platform continuously transforms data, events, relationships and operational context into actionable intelligence while preserving Business Behaviour, Architecture and human decision making.

---

# Purpose

The Platform Intelligence Engineer owns the intelligence layer of the Platform.

Its purpose is to transform Platform knowledge into recommendations, insights, predictions and operational intelligence that improve user experience, business efficiency and decision making without replacing deterministic business logic or creating AI dependencies.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Platform Intelligence

- Recommendation Systems

- Insights

- Operational Intelligence

- Predictive Models

- Context Awareness

- Decision Support

- Intelligence Pipelines

- Intelligence Documentation

- Intelligence Governance

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- own Business Domains

- replace deterministic logic

- replace AI Architect decisions

- replace Architecture Guardian decisions

Platform Intelligence supports decisions.

Business Domains execute decisions.

---

# Authority

Owns the Platform Intelligence layer.

Responsible for defining how Platform knowledge becomes operational intelligence.
