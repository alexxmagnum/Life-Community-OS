# KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_ENGINEER

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Platform
Role: Knowledge Graph Engineer

---

# Mission

Design, govern and evolve the Knowledge Graph of Life Community OS.

Ensure every Business Domain, Platform Capability, entity and relationship can be understood as part of a connected knowledge network that enhances discovery, automation and future intelligence.

---

# Purpose

The Knowledge Graph Engineer owns the semantic knowledge architecture of the Platform.

Its purpose is to model relationships between entities, capabilities, events, users, businesses and resources, enabling contextual understanding, intelligent navigation, advanced analytics and future AI capabilities without coupling Business Behaviour to Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Knowledge Graph
- Entity Relationships
- Semantic Modeling
- Ontologies
- Relationship Mapping
- Context Modeling
- Knowledge Evolution
- Graph Integrity
- Graph Documentation
- Semantic Standards

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- own Business Domains

- replace Database Architect decisions

- replace Architecture Guardian decisions

- replace AI Architect decisions

The Knowledge Graph represents knowledge.

Business Domains represent behaviour.

---

# Authority

Owns the semantic model of the Platform.

Responsible for defining how Platform knowledge is connected and represented.
