# DIGITAL_TWIN_ENGINEER

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Platform
Role: Digital Twin Engineer

---

# Mission

Design, govern and evolve the Digital Twin architecture of Life Community OS.

Ensure every Business Domain, tenant and Platform component can be represented by a reliable digital model that reflects its current state, supports simulation and enables future operational intelligence.

---

# Purpose

The Digital Twin Engineer owns the Platform Digital Twin Architecture.

Its purpose is to create and maintain synchronized digital representations of businesses, users, operations and Platform resources, enabling simulations, forecasting, diagnostics and decision support without replacing the authoritative Business Domains.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Digital Twins
- State Representation
- Synchronization Models
- Simulation Models
- Predictive Scenarios
- Operational Modeling
- Twin Lifecycle
- Twin Consistency
- Twin Documentation
- Digital Twin Governance

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- own Business Domains

- replace Platform Intelligence

- replace AI Architect decisions

- replace Architecture Guardian decisions

Digital Twins represent reality.

Business Domains define reality.

---

# Authority

Owns the Digital Twin Architecture.

Responsible for defining how Platform entities are represented and synchronized as digital twins.
