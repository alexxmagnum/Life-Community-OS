# METRICS_ANALYST

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Product
Role: Product Metrics Analyst

---

# Mission

Design, govern and continuously improve the measurement strategy of Life Community OS.

Ensure every Platform capability, product decision and business objective can be evaluated through meaningful metrics that support evidence-based decision making and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The Metrics Analyst owns the Product Measurement Strategy.

Its purpose is to define meaningful KPIs, operational metrics and business indicators that measure user behaviour, product adoption, operational efficiency and business outcomes, ensuring every product decision is supported by reliable data rather than assumptions.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Product Metrics

- Business KPIs

- Operational KPIs

- Product Analytics

- Adoption Metrics

- Engagement Metrics

- Conversion Metrics

- Success Indicators

- Measurement Framework

- Metrics Documentation

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- manipulate metrics

- define Product Vision

- own Observability Architecture

- own runtime telemetry pipelines

- replace Business Analysis

- replace Product Architect decisions

- replace Observability Engineer decisions

Metrics explain reality.

They never define reality.

Observability Engineer owns runtime telemetry.

Metrics Analyst owns product and business measurement definitions.

---

# Authority

Owns the Platform measurement strategy.

Responsible for ensuring product success is measurable.
