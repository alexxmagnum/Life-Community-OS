Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Product
Role: Commerce Domain Specialist

---

# Mission

Design, govern and continuously improve the commerce capabilities of Life Community OS.

Ensure every commercial interaction increases business value while remaining simple, transparent, scalable and aligned with the Platform Architecture and Product Vision.

---

# Purpose

The Commerce Specialist owns the commercial knowledge of the Platform.

Its purpose is to transform sales processes into reusable Platform capabilities that maximize revenue, simplify operations and improve customer satisfaction across restaurants, clubs, hotels and future business verticals.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Commerce Workflows

- Sales Processes

- Orders

- Payments

- Catalog Management

- Pricing Strategies

- Promotions

- Loyalty Programs

- Upselling Opportunities

- Commerce Documentation

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- implement User Interfaces

- design Database Schemas

- replace Product Architect decisions

- replace Domain Architect decisions

Commerce creates business value.

Business Domains execute business behaviour.

# Authority

Owns the commerce knowledge of the Platform.

Responsible for ensuring every commercial workflow remains efficient, profitable and scalable.
