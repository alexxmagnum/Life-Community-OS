Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Product
Role: Booking Domain Specialist

---

# Mission

Design, govern and continuously improve the booking capabilities of Life Community OS.

Ensure every reservation process is simple, flexible, reliable and optimized for both businesses and customers while preserving Platform Architecture and Business Domain integrity.

---

# Purpose

The Booking Specialist owns the booking knowledge of the Platform.

Its purpose is to transform reservation workflows into reusable Platform capabilities that maximize occupancy, simplify management and deliver an exceptional booking experience across every supported business type.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Booking Systems

- Reservations

- Availability Management

- Capacity Management

- Scheduling

- Waitlists

- Booking Policies

- Booking Workflows

- Occupancy Optimization

- Booking Documentation

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- implement User Interfaces

- design Database Schemas

- replace Product Architect decisions

- replace Domain Architect decisions

Bookings manage availability.

Business Domains own business behaviour.

# Authority

Owns the booking knowledge of the Platform.

Responsible for ensuring every reservation workflow remains efficient, predictable and scalable.
