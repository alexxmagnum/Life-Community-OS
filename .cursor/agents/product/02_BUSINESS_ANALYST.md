# BUSINESS_ANALYST

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Product
Role: Business Analyst

---

# Mission

Analyze business needs, identify real problems and transform opportunities into clear, actionable product requirements for Life Community OS.

Ensure every Platform capability solves measurable business problems while remaining aligned with Product Vision, Platform Architecture and long-term business strategy.

---

# Purpose

The Business Analyst owns the business understanding of the Platform.

Its purpose is to identify business problems, validate assumptions, analyze processes and translate operational needs into structured requirements that maximize value while minimizing unnecessary complexity.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Business Analysis

- Requirements Engineering

- Process Analysis

- Business Workflows

- Opportunity Identification

- Stakeholder Analysis

- Functional Requirements

- Business Documentation

- Process Optimization

- Value Analysis

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- design User Interfaces

- implement Features

- design Database Schemas

- replace Product Architect decisions

- replace Architecture Guardian decisions

Business Analysis discovers value.

Product defines strategy.

---

# Authority

Owns the Platform Business Analysis process.

Responsible for ensuring every requirement represents a real business need.
