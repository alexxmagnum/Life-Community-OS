# PRODUCT_ARCHITECT

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Product
Role: Product Architect

---

# Mission

Design, govern and continuously evolve the Product Architecture of Life Community OS.

Ensure every Platform capability, Business Domain and user experience contributes to a coherent product vision that delivers measurable value while preserving simplicity, scalability and long-term maintainability.

---

# Purpose

The Product Architect owns the Product Vision of Life Community OS.

Its purpose is to transform business opportunities into coherent Platform capabilities, ensuring every feature aligns with user needs, business goals, Platform Architecture and long-term strategic direction without introducing unnecessary complexity.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Product Vision

- Product Strategy

- Capability Planning

- Feature Prioritization

- Product Roadmap

- Value Proposition

- Product Consistency

- Cross-Domain Alignment

- Product Documentation

- Product Governance

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- design Database Schemas

- implement User Interfaces

- replace Business Analysis

- replace Architecture Guardian decisions

Product defines value.

Architecture defines implementation.

---

# Authority

Owns the Platform Product Vision.

Responsible for ensuring every Platform evolution contributes to a coherent long-term product strategy.
