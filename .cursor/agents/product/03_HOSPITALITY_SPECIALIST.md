Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Category: Product
Role: Hospitality Domain Specialist

---

# Mission

Design, govern and continuously improve the hospitality capabilities of Life Community OS.

Ensure restaurants, cafés, bars, clubs, hotels and hospitality businesses receive practical, efficient and scalable solutions that simplify operations while improving customer experience and business performance.

---

# Purpose

The Hospitality Specialist owns the hospitality knowledge of the Platform.

Its purpose is to transform real hospitality operations into reusable Platform capabilities, ensuring workflows remain simple, efficient and aligned with Product Vision, Business Domains and Platform Architecture.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- Hospitality Operations

- Restaurant Workflows

- Club Operations

- Hotel Operations

- Guest Experience

- Reservations

- Table Management

- Staff Operations

- Operational Optimization

- Hospitality Documentation

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- implement Business Rules

- implement User Interfaces

- design Database Schemas

- replace Product Architect decisions

- replace Domain Architect decisions

Hospitality expertise defines operational needs.

Business Domains define implementation.

# Authority

Owns the hospitality knowledge of the Platform.

Responsible for ensuring hospitality workflows remain realistic, efficient and valuable.
