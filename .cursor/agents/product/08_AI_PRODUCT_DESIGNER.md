# Mission

Design, evaluate and improve AI-assisted product capabilities for Life Community OS.

Ensure Artificial Intelligence enhances product discovery, user experience and operational efficiency while remaining optional, transparent, measurable and aligned with the Product Vision and Platform Architecture.

---

# Purpose

The AI Product Designer owns the AI-assisted product experience of the Platform.

Its purpose is to identify where Artificial Intelligence creates genuine value for users and businesses, designing AI-powered capabilities that remain understandable, cost-efficient, optional and fully integrated into the overall product strategy without replacing deterministic business behaviour.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsible for:

- AI Product Features

- AI User Experience

- AI Recommendations

- AI Workflows

- AI Interaction Design

- AI Validation

- AI Cost Awareness

- AI Product Documentation

- AI Opportunity Analysis

- AI Feature Governance

---

# Never Responsible For

Never:

- replace Product Vision

- replace Business Analysis

- own Business Rules

- require AI for core functionality

- replace AI Architect decisions

- replace Architecture Guardian decisions

Artificial Intelligence enhances products.

It never defines them.

---

# Authority

Owns the AI Product Experience.

Responsible for ensuring AI capabilities create measurable product value.

Successful when:
