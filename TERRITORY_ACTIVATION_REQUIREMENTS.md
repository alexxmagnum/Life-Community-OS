# Territory Activation Requirements — Phase 18M-FIX-A

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Principle:** LIFE does not fabricate activity. Real territories need real seed content from people and institutions.

---

## Minimum viable territory (legitimate seed)

| Requirement | Source | Why |
|-------------|--------|-----|
| **Lugares reales** | Admin territory / map | Gives Discover and Life Place context |
| **Información pública** | Location profiles, hours, contact | Trust for visitors before membership |
| **Primer aviso** | Admin or authorized member | Official/maintenance signal — not fake |
| **Primera experiencia** | Member organizer | Proves CREATE → DISCOVER → PARTICIPATE loop |
| **Negocio o servicio publicado** | Owner + admin review | Local economy presence (lifecycle trust) |
| **Residentes invitados** | Admin members | Density without bot accounts |

---

## What NOT to seed

- Fake experiences or auto-generated events
- Fictitious users or bot participants
- Synthetic engagement (likes, rankings)
- Auto-posts to simulate a social wall

---

## Activation playbook (human-operated)

1. Configure territory places and public info.
2. Publish one official announcement (maintenance, welcome, or event info).
3. Support one member to create the first experience.
4. Onboard one local business through review lifecycle.
5. Invite residents via membership (codes/invitations).
6. Monitor **Actividad comunitaria** metrics — real counts only.

---

## Empty community UX (product)

When no seed exists yet:

- Empty states invite **creation** with contextual CTAs → Magic Plus (focused intent).
- `CommunityActivationPanel` orients members without inventing content.
- Visitors see **Únete para participar** — not fake activity.

---

## Success criteria

A territory is **activation-ready** when:

- At least 1 published experience OR reservation is joinable
- At least 1 public announcement OR official notice exists
- Discover shows real places (+ published businesses if any)
- Admin dashboard shows non-zero activity metrics from real actions
