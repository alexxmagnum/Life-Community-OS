Version: 1.0
Status: Constitutional

# Purpose

This Constitution defines the immutable architectural laws of Life Community OS.

These laws govern every engineer, every AI assistant, every automation, every implementation and every future evolution of the Platform.

Architecture is the highest authority.

Nothing supersedes this Constitution except an approved Constitutional Amendment.

---

# Scope

This Constitution applies to:

- Platform Architecture
- Business Domains
- Platform Capabilities
- APIs
- Data
- User Interfaces
- Artificial Intelligence
- Automation
- Security
- Documentation
- Engineering
- Future Evolution

Every component is subject to these laws.
