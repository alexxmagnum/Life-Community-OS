# 06_AUDITING

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Document Type: Data Architecture
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Auditing Architecture of Life Community OS.

Auditing provides complete historical traceability of Business Entities while preserving integrity, accountability and long-term observability.

Auditing belongs to the Data Model.

Every Business Entity may generate Audit Information throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS preserve the history of Business Data?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- auditing architecture;
- audit lifecycle;
- audit ownership;
- historical traceability;
- governance.

It does not define:

- logging;
- monitoring;
- observability infrastructure;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Auditing records the historical evolution of Business Entities.

Auditing explains what happened.

It never changes Business Data.

---

# Objectives

Auditing exists to:

- preserve historical information;
- support accountability;
- simplify investigations;
- improve transparency;
- support compliance;
- enable long-term traceability.

---

# Auditing Philosophy

Business Data changes.

Business History remains.

Nothing important should become impossible to explain.

---

# Actors

Audit events may be generated by:

Artificial Intelligence

---

# Immutability

Audit history should never be modified.

Corrections generate new audit events.

Historical information remains preserved.

---

# Ownership

Audit events belong to the same ownership context as the audited entity.

Platform Data generates Platform Audit.

Tenant Data generates Tenant Audit.

Ownership remains explicit.

---

# Business Independence

Business Domains never implement auditing.

Audit generation belongs to the platform.

Business behaviour remains independent.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence generates Audit Events like every other actor.

AI never bypasses auditing.

---

# Automation

Automation generates Audit Events.

Automation remains fully traceable.

---

# Security

Security protects Audit Information.

Audit history should never weaken Security.

---

# Performance

Audit generation should remain lightweight.

Optimization never sacrifices traceability.

---

# Observability

Auditing supports Observability by exposing:

Entity History

Actor History

Lifecycle Changes

Ownership Changes

Business Timeline

Audit remains observable.

---

# Product Rules

Auditing belongs to the Data Model.

Audit history remains immutable.

Business Entities generate historical records.

Architecture remains deterministic.

---

# Relationship With Entities

Entities generate Audit Events.

Audit records entity history.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Observability

Observability explains runtime behaviour.

Auditing explains historical behaviour.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Security

Security protects Audit Information.

Auditing preserves historical information.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Governance

Future Auditing capabilities should preserve:

- immutability;
- chronological history;
- deterministic behaviour;
- explicit ownership;
- architectural simplicity.

Major architectural changes require ADR documentation.

---

# Future Evolution

Future versions may introduce:

Entity Timelines;

Business Replay;

Audit Analytics;

Compliance Dashboards;

Event Correlation;

Immutable Audit Storage.

These capabilities should preserve Auditing architecture.

---

# Success Criteria

Auditing is successful when:

every important Business Action becomes traceable;

history remains immutable;

Business Domains remain audit-independent;

future compliance requires no redesign;

architecture remains stable.

---

# Conclusion

Auditing preserves the complete historical evolution of Business Entities across Life Community OS.

Business changes.

History remains.

Architecture remains stable.
