# 15_DATA_EVOLUTION

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Document Type: Data Architecture
Priority: High

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution of the Data Model inside Life Community OS.

Data Evolution ensures that Business Data can continuously evolve while preserving Business Reality, architectural consistency and long-term compatibility.

Data Evolution belongs to the Data Model.

---

# Question this document answers

> How should the Data Model evolve over time without redesigning the platform?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- long-term data evolution;
- future modeling capabilities;
- architectural continuity;
- governance principles;
- scalability strategy.

It does not define:

- storage technologies;
- implementation details;
- infrastructure;
- database migrations.

---

# Definition

Data Evolution is the controlled expansion of the Data Model while preserving Business Reality and architectural stability.

Business evolves.

Architecture remains stable.

---

# Objectives

Data Evolution exists to:

- support future Business Domains;
- preserve compatibility;
- simplify expansion;
- reduce redesign;
- enable innovation;
- maintain long-term consistency.

---

# Evolution Philosophy

The Data Model evolves by expanding capabilities.

Never by redesigning Business Reality.

Existing Business Entities remain valid.

---

# Architectural Stability

The following principles should never change:

Business Reality defines the model.

Business Entities represent concepts.

Relationships represent reality.

Identifiers remain permanent.

Ownership remains explicit.

Technology remains replaceable.

These principles remain permanent.
