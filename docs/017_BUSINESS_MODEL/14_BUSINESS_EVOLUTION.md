# Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution strategy of the Business Platform inside Life Community OS.

The Business Platform continuously evolves to support new commercial models, revenue streams and ecosystem capabilities while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Evolution belongs to the Business Platform.

Business Domains remain commercially independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How should the Business Platform evolve without redesigning the architecture?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- long-term business evolution;
- commercial evolution;
- platform growth;
- ecosystem expansion;
- governance.

It does not define:

- marketing strategy;
- financial planning;
- accounting;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Business Evolution expands commercial capabilities while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Commercial Models evolve.

The Business Evolution Platform exists to:
