# Purpose

This document defines the Business Governance Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Business Governance Platform establishes the architectural rules, policies and standards governing every commercial capability while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Business Governance belongs to the Business Platform.

Every commercial capability follows Governance.

---

# Question this document answers

> How is the Business Platform governed across Life Community OS?

# Scope

This document defines:

- business governance;
- commercial governance;
- capability governance;
- policy governance;
- long-term evolution.

It does not define:

- accounting;
- taxation;
- implementation details;
- legal contracts.

# Definition

Business Governance defines the permanent architectural rules governing the commercial platform.

Governance preserves consistency.

Technology implements governance.

# Objectives

The Business Governance Platform exists to:

- preserve architectural consistency;
- standardize commercial capabilities;
- simplify commercial evolution;
- maximize Business Capability reuse;
- reduce technical debt;
- support long-term scalability.
