# Purpose

This document defines the long-term Business Vision of Life Community OS.

The Business Platform exists to provide a universal commercial foundation capable of supporting unlimited industries, brands, business models and monetization strategies while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

The vision belongs to the Business Platform.

Business Domains remain commercially independent.
