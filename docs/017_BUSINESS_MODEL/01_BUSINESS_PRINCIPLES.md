# 01_BUSINESS_PRINCIPLES

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Document Type: Business Platform Architecture
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent Business Platform Principles of Life Community OS.

These principles establish the architectural foundation governing every commercial capability across the platform.

Commercial Models evolve.

Business Principles remain stable.

---

# Question this document answers

> Which principles govern the Business Platform inside Life Community OS?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- business philosophy;
- commercial principles;
- entitlement principles;
- monetization principles;
- long-term evolution.

It does not define:

- pricing;
- taxation;
- accounting;
- payment providers.

---

# Definition

Business Platform Principles define the permanent architectural rules governing every commercial capability.

Every commercial model follows these principles.

---

# Objectives

The Business Platform Principles exist to:

- preserve commercial consistency;
- simplify monetization;
- reduce coupling;
- maximize capability reuse;
- support white-label products;
- enable long-term scalability.

---

# Principle 1

Business Behaviour belongs to Business Domains.

Commercial Behaviour belongs to the Business Platform.

---

# Principle 2

Capabilities are independent from pricing.

Pricing never changes Business Behaviour.

---

# Principle 3

Commercial Products define what is sold.

They never implement Business Behaviour.

---

# Principle 4

Subscriptions define the commercial relationship.

They never define Business Logic.

---

# Principle 5

Entitlements determine what a customer may use.

Business Domains consume entitlements.

---

# Principle 6

Business Capabilities remain reusable.

Commercial Models consume capabilities.

---

# Principle 7

Tenants remain commercially isolated.

Commercial changes never affect other tenants.

---

# Principle 8

Commercial configuration remains data-driven.

Business Behaviour should never depend on hardcoded plans.

---

# Principle 9

White-label products reuse the same Business Platform.

Branding never changes Business Behaviour.

---

# Principle 10

Marketplace integrations consume Business Capabilities.

Business Domains remain unchanged.

---

# Principle 11

Artificial Intelligence may improve commercial decisions.

AI never changes contractual rights automatically.

---

# Principle 12

Automation simplifies commercial workflows.

Automation remains observable.

---

# Principle 13

Technology remains replaceable.

Commercial Architecture remains stable.

---

# Principle 14

Commercial Models continuously evolve.

Business Behaviour remains unchanged.

---

# Principle 15

Architecture outlives pricing.

Business Principles remain permanent.

---

# Business Constitutional Rules

Business Behaviour remains deterministic.

Commercial Behaviour remains configurable.

Capabilities remain reusable.

Security remains centralized.

Tenant isolation remains mandatory.

Architecture remains stable.

---

# Relationship With Business Domains

Business Domains provide Business Behaviour.

The Business Platform governs commercial access.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Security

Security validates identity.

The Business Platform validates commercial rights.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Platform Architecture

Platform Architecture defines technical foundations.

Business Principles define commercial foundations.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Governance

Future Business Platform capabilities should preserve:

- commercial independence;
- technology independence;
- reusable capabilities;
- deterministic behaviour;
- architectural simplicity;
- long-term scalability.

Major architectural changes require ADR documentation.

---

# Future Evolution

Future principles may introduce:

Consumption-Based Pricing;

AI Commercial Assistance;

Partner Revenue Models;

Dynamic Entitlements;

Commercial Automation;

Future Monetization Models.

These capabilities should preserve existing principles.

---

# Success Criteria

The Business Platform Principles are successful when:

commercial models evolve independently;

Business Domains remain commercially independent;

pricing changes require no Business redesign;

architecture remains stable.

---

# Conclusion

The Business Platform Principles define the permanent philosophy governing every commercial capability inside Life Community OS.

Commercial Models evolve.

Business Behaviour remains stable.

Architecture remains timeless.

---
