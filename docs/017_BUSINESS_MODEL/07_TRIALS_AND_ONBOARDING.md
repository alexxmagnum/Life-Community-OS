# Purpose

This document defines the Trials and Onboarding Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Trials and Onboarding Platform governs how new Tenants discover, evaluate and activate Commercial Products while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Trials and Onboarding belong to the Business Platform.

Business Domains remain onboarding-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does a new customer become an active customer?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- trial architecture;
- onboarding lifecycle;
- activation flow;
- evaluation process;
- governance.

It does not define:

- marketing campaigns;
- payment providers;
- UI implementation;
- sales processes.

---

# Definition

Trials and Onboarding represent the commercial journey from first contact to active subscription.

Business Behaviour remains unchanged.

Commercial state evolves.

---

# Objectives

The Trials and Onboarding Platform exists to:

- reduce adoption friction;
- accelerate activation;
- improve conversion;
- simplify configuration;
- maximize retention;
- support long-term scalability.
