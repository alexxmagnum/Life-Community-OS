Version: 1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the White Label Architecture of Life Community OS.

The White Label Platform enables multiple commercial brands to operate on the same Business Platform while preserving Business Behaviour, Security and architectural consistency.

White Label belongs to the Business Platform.

Business Domains remain brand-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS support multiple commercial brands?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- white-label architecture;
- brand separation;
- identity management;
- tenant branding;
- governance.

It does not define:

- UI implementation;
- hosting;
- domains;
- infrastructure.

---

# Definition

White Label enables different commercial identities to reuse the same platform capabilities.

Brands differ.

Business Behaviour remains identical.

---

# Objectives

The White Label Platform exists to:

- support multiple brands;
- maximize platform reuse;
- simplify commercialization;
- reduce maintenance;
- enable partner ecosystems;
- support long-term scalability.

---

# White Label Philosophy

Business Behaviour belongs to Business Domains.

Brand Identity belongs to the Business Platform.

Appearance changes.

Behaviour remains unchanged.

# Relationship With Tenants

Tenants belong to Brands.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Commercial Products

Brands may expose different Commercial Products.

Commercial Products remain reusable.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Marketplace

Marketplace Assets may be Brand-specific.

Marketplace never changes Business Behaviour.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Governance

Future White Label capabilities should preserve:

- reusable Business Capabilities;
- configurable Brand Identity;
- technology independence;
- deterministic behaviour;
- long-term scalability.

Major architectural changes require ADR documentation.

---

# Success Criteria

The White Label Platform is successful when:

new Brands require no Business redesign;

Business Domains remain brand-independent;

Brand Identity remains configurable;

future Brands integrate naturally;

architecture remains stable.

# Conclusion

The White Label Platform enables multiple commercial identities while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Brands evolve.
