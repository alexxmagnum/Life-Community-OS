# Purpose

This document defines the Pricing Model Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Pricing Model determines how Commercial Products are valued while preserving Business Behaviour, commercial flexibility and architectural consistency.

Pricing belongs to the Business Platform.

Business Domains remain pricing-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS price Commercial Products?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- pricing architecture;
- pricing strategies;
- pricing lifecycle;
- commercial flexibility;
- governance.

It does not define:

- payment providers;
- taxation;
- accounting;
- invoicing.

---

# Definition

The Pricing Model determines the monetary value associated with Commercial Products.

Pricing defines value.

It never defines Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

The Pricing Model exists to:

- support multiple pricing strategies;
- simplify commercial evolution;
- separate pricing from capabilities;
- maximize product reuse;
- support multiple markets;
- enable long-term scalability.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Capabilities create value.

Commercial Products package value.

Pricing monetizes value.

Business Behaviour remains unchanged.
