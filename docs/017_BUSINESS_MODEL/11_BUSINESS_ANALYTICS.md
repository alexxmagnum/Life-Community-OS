# Purpose

This document defines the Business Analytics Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Business Analytics Platform provides commercial insights, business intelligence and strategic metrics while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Business Analytics belongs to the Business Platform.

Business Domains remain analytics-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS understand the health of its commercial platform?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- business analytics architecture;
- commercial metrics;
- revenue analytics;
- growth analytics;
- governance.

It does not define:

- BI tools;
- dashboards;
- data warehouses;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Business Analytics transforms commercial activity into strategic insights.

Analytics explain commercial behaviour.

They never modify Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

The Business Analytics Platform exists to:

- understand platform growth;
- improve commercial decisions;
- measure product adoption;
- optimize monetization;
- detect commercial opportunities;
- support long-term scalability.

---

# Business Analytics Philosophy

Everything measurable can improve.

Business Analytics supports decisions.

It never executes Business Behaviour.
