# Purpose

This document defines the Domain Expansion Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Domain Expansion Platform governs how new Business Domains are introduced while preserving Platform Stability, Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Domain Expansion belongs to Product Strategy.

Platform Capabilities remain reusable.

---

# Question this document answers

> How can Life Community OS expand into new industries without redesigning the platform?

This document defines:

- domain evolution;
- industry expansion;
- capability reuse;
- strategic growth;
- governance.

- implementation details;
- commercial plans;
- sprint planning;
- release scheduling.

# Definition

A Business Domain is a collection of Business Behaviour built on reusable Platform Capabilities.

Domains evolve.

Platform Capabilities remain stable.

# Objectives

The Domain Expansion Platform exists to:

- support unlimited industries;
- maximize capability reuse;
- minimize duplicated logic;
- preserve platform consistency;
- simplify future growth;
- enable long-term scalability.
