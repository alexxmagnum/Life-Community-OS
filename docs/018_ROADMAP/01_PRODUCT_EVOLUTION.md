# Purpose

This document defines the Product Evolution Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Product Evolution Platform governs how the platform continuously expands through reusable capabilities while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Product Evolution belongs to Product Strategy.

Business Domains remain evolution-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How should Life Community OS evolve over the coming years?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- product evolution;
- capability evolution;
- platform evolution;
- architectural continuity;
- governance.

It does not define:

- sprint planning;
- project management;
- implementation details;
- delivery schedules.

# Definition

Product Evolution continuously expands platform capabilities without redesigning the Business Platform.

Architecture remains stable.

The Product Evolution Platform exists to:
