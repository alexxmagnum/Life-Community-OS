# Purpose

This document defines the Product Metrics Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Product Metrics Platform measures the health, maturity and evolution of the platform while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Product Metrics belong to Product Strategy.

Business Domains remain metrics-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How do we objectively measure the evolution and health of Life Community OS?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- product metrics;
- capability metrics;
- platform health;
- roadmap metrics;
- governance.

It does not define:

- business analytics;
- financial KPIs;
- implementation details;
- dashboard implementation.

---

# Definition

Product Metrics transform platform activity into measurable indicators of product quality and maturity.

Metrics inform decisions.

Metrics never change Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

The Product Metrics Platform exists to:

- measure platform maturity;
- evaluate capability adoption;
- monitor roadmap progress;
- reduce technical debt;
- improve decision making;
- support long-term scalability.
