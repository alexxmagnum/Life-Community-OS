# Purpose

This document defines the Risk Management Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Risk Management Platform identifies, evaluates and mitigates strategic, architectural and operational risks while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Risk Management belongs to Product Strategy.

Business Domains remain risk-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS identify and manage risks throughout its evolution?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- strategic risk management;
- architectural risks;
- operational risks;
- product risks;
- governance.

It does not define:

- incident response;
- disaster recovery;
- security operations;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Risk Management continuously evaluates threats that may compromise platform evolution.

Risks influence decisions.

Risks never define Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

The Risk Management Platform exists to:

- preserve platform stability;
- reduce technical debt;
- detect architectural risks early;
- improve decision quality;
- support sustainable growth;
- enable long-term scalability.

---

# Risk Philosophy

Every decision introduces risk.

Every risk should be measurable.

Every mitigation should be intentional.

Architecture remains protected.
