# Purpose

This document defines the Automation Roadmap Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Automation Roadmap governs the long-term evolution of reusable automation capabilities while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Automation evolves.

Business Behaviour remains deterministic.

# Definition

Automation is a reusable Platform Capability responsible for orchestrating Business Operations.

Automation coordinates.

Business Domains execute.

# Objectives

The Automation Roadmap exists to:

- eliminate repetitive work;
- improve operational efficiency;
- orchestrate platform capabilities;
- simplify integrations;
- support AI;
- enable long-term scalability.

# Success Criteria

The Automation Roadmap is successful when:

Business Domains remain automation-independent;

new workflows require minimal implementation effort;

automation remains observable;

architecture remains valid for decades.
