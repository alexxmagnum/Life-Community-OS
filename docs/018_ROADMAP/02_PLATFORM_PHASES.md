# Purpose

This document defines the Platform Phases Architecture of Life Community OS.

Platform Phases describe the strategic maturity of the platform while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Platform Phases belong to Product Strategy.

Business Domains remain phase-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does the platform mature over time?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- platform maturity;
- strategic phases;
- capability progression;
- roadmap milestones;
- governance.

It does not define:

- software versions;
- sprint planning;
- release dates;
- implementation details.

Platform Phases describe the evolution of the platform through increasing architectural maturity.
