# Purpose

This document defines the Product Governance Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Product Governance Platform establishes the principles, policies and decision framework that guide the long-term evolution of the platform while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Governance belongs to Product Strategy.

Business Domains remain governance-independent.
