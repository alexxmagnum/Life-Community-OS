# Purpose

This document defines the Capability Roadmap Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Capability Roadmap governs how Platform Capabilities evolve independently while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Capabilities belong to the Platform.

Business Domains consume Capabilities.

---

# Question this document answers

> How do Platform Capabilities evolve independently over time?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- capability evolution;
- capability maturity;
- capability dependencies;
- capability planning;
- governance.

It does not define:

- sprint planning;
- software versions;
- project management;
- implementation details.

# Definition

Platform Capabilities are reusable platform assets that evolve independently from Business Domains.

Capabilities expand.

Business Behaviour remains deterministic.

---

# Objectives

The Capability Roadmap exists to:

- maximize capability reuse;
- simplify platform evolution;
- reduce architectural redesign;
- organize platform growth;
- preserve Business Behaviour;
- support long-term scalability.
