# Purpose

This document defines the Product Roadmap Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Product Roadmap defines how the platform evolves over time while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and Architectural Consistency.

The Roadmap belongs to Product Strategy.

Business Domains remain roadmap-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS evolve without redesigning the platform?

This document defines:

- long-term strategy.
