# Purpose

This document defines the Progressive Platform Observability Architecture of Life Community OS.

Progressive Platform Observability provides visibility into the health, behaviour and effectiveness of Progressive Capabilities while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Observability belongs to the Progressive Platform.

Business Domains remain observability-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS understand the behaviour of its Progressive Platform?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- progressive observability;
- capability monitoring;
- health monitoring;
- operational visibility;
- governance.

It does not define:

- logging systems;
- monitoring infrastructure;
- telemetry providers;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Progressive Platform Observability continuously measures the health and behaviour of Progressive Capabilities.

Observability explains the platform.

It never changes platform behaviour.

---

# Objectives

Progressive Platform Observability exists to:

- improve reliability;
- detect operational issues;
- monitor user experience;
- simplify troubleshooting;
- support optimization;
- enable long-term evolution.

---

# Observability Philosophy

Progressive Capabilities should never become invisible.

Everything important should be measurable.

Understanding precedes optimization.
