# 01_PWA_PRINCIPLES

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Document Type: Progressive Platform Architecture
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent Progressive Platform Principles of Life Community OS.

These principles establish the architectural foundation governing every Progressive Capability across the platform.

Technology evolves.

Browsers evolve.

User Experience remains.

---

# Question this document answers

> Which principles govern every Progressive Capability inside Life Community OS?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- progressive philosophy;
- offline principles;
- resilience principles;
- user experience principles;
- long-term evolution.

It does not define:

- browser APIs;
- implementation details;
- infrastructure;
- service workers.

---

# Definition

Progressive Platform Principles define the permanent architectural rules governing every Progressive Capability.

Every Progressive Feature follows these principles.

---

# Objectives

The Progressive Platform Principles exist to:

- preserve architectural consistency;
- improve User Experience;
- simplify evolution;
- reduce technology coupling;
- support long-term scalability;
- guarantee resilience.

---

# Principle 1

User Experience comes first.

Technology supports the experience.

Technology never defines the experience.

---

# Principle 2

Connectivity improves the platform.

Connectivity should never become a mandatory requirement whenever avoidable.

---

# Principle 3

Offline capability is a platform feature.

Not a module feature.

---

# Principle 4

Synchronization should be automatic whenever possible.

Users should not manage synchronization manually.

---

# Principle 5

Local work should never be unnecessarily lost.

Business continuity has priority.

---

# Principle 6

Progressive capabilities belong to the platform.

Business Domains consume them.

---

# Principle 7

Installation improves accessibility.

Installation never changes Business Behaviour.

---

# Principle 8

Device capabilities remain optional.

The platform should degrade gracefully when unavailable.

---

# Principle 9

Connectivity should always remain observable.

The platform adapts to connectivity.

Users should not need technical knowledge.

---

# Principle 10

Progressive capabilities remain transparent.

Users focus on their work.

Not on platform mechanics.

---

# Principle 11

Security remains mandatory.

Offline capabilities never bypass Security.

---

# Principle 12

Synchronization preserves Business Consistency.

Business Data remains deterministic.

---

# Principle 13

Business Behaviour never depends on browser capabilities.

The browser provides delivery.

The platform provides behaviour.

---

# Principle 14

Progressive capabilities should remain reusable.

Future platforms should reuse the same architecture.

---

# Principle 15

Architecture remains stable.

Technology evolves.

User Experience improves.

---

# Progressive Constitutional Rules

User Experience comes first.

Connectivity enhances the experience.

Offline remains supported.

Synchronization remains transparent.

Security remains mandatory.

Business Behaviour remains deterministic.

Architecture remains stable.

---

# Relationship With User Experience

User Experience defines interaction.

Progressive capabilities improve delivery.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With API

The API Platform provides Business Capabilities.

The Progressive Platform manages resilience.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Data Model

The Data Model represents Business Reality.

Progressive capabilities temporarily synchronize Business Data.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Relationship With Security

Security protects Business Data.

Progressive capabilities never bypass Security.

Responsibilities remain separated.

---

# Governance

Future Progressive Platform capabilities should preserve:

- user-first architecture;
- technology independence;
- deterministic behaviour;
- architectural simplicity;
- long-term scalability.

Major architectural changes require ADR documentation.

---

# Future Evolution

Future principles may introduce:

offline artificial intelligence;

peer synchronization;

distributed edge computing;

predictive synchronization;

ambient user experiences;

future web capabilities.

These capabilities should preserve existing principles.

---

# Success Criteria

The Progressive Platform Principles are successful when:

User Experience remains consistent;

Business Behaviour remains deterministic;

offline capabilities remain reliable;

future technologies require no redesign;

architecture remains stable.

---

# Conclusion

The Progressive Platform Principles define the permanent philosophy governing every Progressive Capability inside Life Community OS.

Technology evolves.

Browsers evolve.

The principles remain.
