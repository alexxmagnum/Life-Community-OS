Version: 1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution strategy of the Progressive Platform inside Life Community OS.

Progressive Platform Evolution ensures that Progressive Capabilities continuously evolve while preserving User Experience, Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Evolution belongs to the Progressive Platform.

Business Domains remain unaffected by technological evolution.

---

# Question this document answers

> How should the Progressive Platform evolve over time without redesigning the platform?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- long-term evolution;
- future progressive capabilities;
- architectural continuity;
- technology independence;
- governance.

It does not define:

- browser implementations;
- infrastructure;
- operating systems;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Progressive Platform Evolution is the controlled expansion of Progressive Capabilities while preserving architectural stability.

Technology evolves.

Business Behaviour remains stable.

---

# Objectives

Progressive Platform Evolution exists to:

- support future web capabilities;
- simplify platform evolution;
- reduce architectural redesign;
- improve User Experience;
- preserve compatibility;
- support long-term scalability.

---

# Evolution Philosophy

The Progressive Platform evolves by adding capabilities.

Never by changing Business Behaviour.

Business Domains remain unchanged.

---

# Architectural Stability

The following principles should never change:

Business Behaviour remains deterministic.

Progressive Capabilities remain optional.

Connectivity enhances the experience.

Offline remains supported.

Synchronization remains automatic.

Security remains centralized.

Architecture remains reusable.

These principles remain permanent.
