# Purpose

This document defines the Progressive Platform Optimization Architecture of Life Community OS.

Progressive Platform Optimization continuously improves responsiveness, resilience, efficiency and User Experience while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Optimization belongs to the Progressive Platform.

Business Domains remain optimization-independent.
