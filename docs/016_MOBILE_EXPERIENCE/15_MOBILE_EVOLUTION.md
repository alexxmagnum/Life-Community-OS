Version: 1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution strategy of the Mobile Experience Platform inside Life Community OS.

The Mobile Experience Platform continuously evolves to support new devices, interaction models and technologies while preserving Business Behaviour, Security and architectural consistency.

Evolution belongs to the Mobile Experience Platform.

Business Domains remain unaffected by Mobile Experience evolution.

---

# Question this document answers

> How should the Mobile Experience Platform evolve over time without redesigning the platform?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- long-term evolution;
- future mobile capabilities;
- architectural continuity;
- technology independence;
- governance.

It does not define:

- operating systems;
- UI frameworks;
- implementation details;
- infrastructure.

# Definition

The Mobile Experience Platform evolves by extending reusable capabilities while preserving architectural stability.
