# Purpose

This document defines the Mobile Experience Optimization Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Mobile Experience Optimization Platform continuously improves mobile usability, responsiveness and operational efficiency while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Optimization belongs to the Mobile Experience Platform.

Business Domains remain optimization-independent.
