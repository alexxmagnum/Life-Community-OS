# Purpose

This document defines the Mobile Experience Observability Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Mobile Experience Observability Platform provides visibility into mobile behaviour, device usage, user interactions and operational health while preserving Business Behaviour and architectural consistency.

Observability belongs to the Mobile Experience Platform.

Business Domains remain observability-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does Life Community OS understand the quality of the Mobile Experience?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- mobile observability architecture;
- experience monitoring;
- interaction analytics;
- performance visibility;
- governance.

It does not define:

- telemetry infrastructure;
- analytics providers;
- logging implementation;
- monitoring tools.

---

# Definition

Mobile Experience Observability continuously measures how users experience the platform across mobile devices.

Observability explains experiences.

It never changes Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

The Mobile Experience Observability Platform exists to:

- improve User Experience;
- detect usability problems;
- measure performance;
- optimize interactions;
- support continuous evolution;
- enable long-term scalability.

---

# Observability Philosophy

Every important mobile interaction should be measurable.

Understanding precedes optimization.

Visibility enables evolution.
