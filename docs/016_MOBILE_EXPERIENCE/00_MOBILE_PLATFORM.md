# Purpose

This document defines the Mobile Experience Platform Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Mobile Experience Platform provides a unified architecture for delivering Business Experiences across mobile devices while preserving Business Behaviour, Security, User Experience and architectural consistency.

The Mobile Experience Platform belongs to the Experience Layer.

Business Domains remain mobile-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> What is the Mobile Experience Platform inside Life Community OS?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- mobile experience architecture;
- mobile philosophy;
- mobile responsibilities;
- mobile capabilities;
- long-term evolution.

It does not define:

- native applications;
- UI implementation;
- operating systems;
- infrastructure.

---

# Definition

The Mobile Experience Platform provides a consistent Business Experience across mobile devices regardless of the underlying technology.

It delivers experiences.

It never owns Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

The Mobile Experience Platform exists to:

- deliver excellent mobile experiences;
- maximize platform reuse;
- support multiple device types;
- preserve Business Behaviour;
- simplify future evolution;
- support long-term scalability.

---

# Mobile Philosophy

Business Behaviour belongs to Business Domains.
