# Purpose

This document defines the Architecture Decision Records (ADR) system of Life Community OS.

The ADR Platform preserves every architectural decision that influences the long-term evolution of the Platform.

Architectural decisions are permanent assets.

Knowledge remains preserved.

# Question this document answers

> How are architectural decisions documented, governed and preserved?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- Architecture Decision Records;

It does not define:

- implementation details;
- source code;
- project management;
- sprint planning.

---

# Definition

An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) documents an architectural decision that has long-term impact on the Platform.

Every ADR explains:

Why the decision exists.

Why alternatives were rejected.

What consequences exist.

How future decisions should relate to it.

---

# Objectives

The ADR Platform exists to:

- preserve architectural knowledge;
- avoid repeating discussions;
- justify strategic decisions;
- simplify onboarding;
- improve long-term maintainability;
- protect Platform Architecture.
