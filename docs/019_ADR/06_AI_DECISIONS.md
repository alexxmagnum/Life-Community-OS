# 06_AI_DECISIONS

Version: 1.0
Status: Accepted
Document Type: Architecture Decision Records
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent Artificial Intelligence Decisions of Life Community OS.

AI Decisions establish the architectural rules governing Artificial Intelligence while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Artificial Intelligence evolves.

Architecture remains stable.

---

# Question this document answers

> Which architectural decisions permanently govern Artificial Intelligence?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- AI Architecture;
- AI Governance;
- AI Responsibilities;
- AI Boundaries;
- AI Evolution.

It does not define:

- model providers;
- prompt engineering;
- infrastructure;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Artificial Intelligence is a reusable Platform Capability.

Business Domains consume AI.

Artificial Intelligence never owns Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

AI Decisions exist to:

- preserve deterministic Business Behaviour;
- maximize AI reuse;
- reduce AI fragmentation;
- simplify AI evolution;
- improve governance;
- support long-term scalability.

---

# AI Decision 001

Artificial Intelligence belongs to the Platform.

Business Domains consume AI.

---

# AI Decision 002

AI augments decision making.

AI never replaces Business Behaviour.

---

# AI Decision 003

Every AI Capability owns a single responsibility.

AI services remain composable.

---

# AI Decision 004

Business Domains never implement proprietary AI logic directly.

Reusable AI Services remain centralized.

---

# AI Decision 005

AI consumes Platform Knowledge.

Knowledge remains authoritative.

---

# AI Decision 006

Every AI interaction remains observable.

Observability is mandatory.

---

# AI Decision 007

Human approval is required whenever Business Risk exists.

Human oversight remains permanent.

---

# AI Decision 008

AI responses remain explainable whenever possible.

Opaque behaviour is minimized.

---

# AI Decision 009

AI Context remains isolated per Tenant.

Cross-Tenant context is prohibited.

---

# AI Decision 010

AI Capabilities expose explicit contracts.

Hidden behaviour is prohibited.

---

# AI Decision 011

Prompt definitions remain versioned.

AI behaviour evolves intentionally.

---

# AI Decision 012

AI consumes Platform Permissions.

AI never bypasses Security.

---

# AI Decision 013

AI consumes Platform Automation.

Automation executes.

AI reasons.

Responsibilities remain separated.

# AI Decision 015

---

# AI Decision 016

Providers evolve.

Architecture remains stable.

---

# AI Decision 017

AI cost remains measurable.

Efficiency is continuously optimized.

---

# AI Decision 018

Every AI Capability remains independently testable.

Evaluation remains measurable.

---

# AI Decision 019

AI continuously improves through Platform Knowledge.

Business Behaviour remains deterministic.

---

# AI Decision 020

Artificial Intelligence evolves independently from Business Domains.

Architecture remains reusable.
