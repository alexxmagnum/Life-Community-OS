# 13_GOVERNANCE_DECISIONS

Version: 1.0
Status: Accepted
Document Type: Architecture Decision Records
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent Governance Decisions of Life Community OS.

Governance Decisions establish the architectural rules governing Platform Evolution while preserving Business Behaviour, Platform Stability and architectural consistency.

Governance evolves.

Architecture remains stable.

---

# Question this document answers

> Which architectural decisions permanently govern Platform Evolution?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- architectural governance;
- decision governance;
- capability governance;
- roadmap governance;
- change governance.

It does not define:

- project management;
- sprint planning;
- implementation details;
- operational procedures.

---

# Definition

Governance preserves the integrity of the Platform while enabling continuous evolution.

Governance protects Architecture.

Innovation expands the Platform.

---

# Objectives

Governance Decisions exist to:

- preserve architectural consistency;
- reduce technical debt;
- improve decision quality;
- maximize capability reuse;
- simplify long-term evolution;
- support sustainable growth.

---

# Governance Decision 001

Architecture governs Technology.

Technology never governs Architecture.

---

# Governance Decision 002

Every Platform Capability follows Platform Architecture.

Capabilities never define Architecture.

---

# Governance Decision 003

Every significant architectural change requires an ADR.

Undocumented strategic decisions are prohibited.

---

# Governance Decision 004

Every reusable capability belongs to the Platform.

Business Domains consume capabilities.

---

# Governance Decision 005

Every Capability declares ownership.

Ownership remains explicit.

---

# Governance Decision 006

Every architectural decision remains traceable.

Decision history is preserved permanently.

---

# Governance Decision 007

Platform evolution is capability-driven.

Platform evolution never becomes feature-driven.

---

# Governance Decision 008

Configuration is preferred over customization.

Configuration preserves Platform consistency.

---

# Governance Decision 009

Every architectural decision evaluates:

Business Value

Platform Value

Capability Reuse

Architectural Impact

Risk

Long-Term Sustainability

Decision quality remains measurable.

---

# Governance Decision 010

Every Platform Capability remains observable.

Governance requires visibility.

---

# Governance Decision 011

Every Platform Capability declares its lifecycle.

Lifecycle remains governed.

---

# Governance Decision 012

Technical Debt remains measurable.

Debt reduction remains continuous.

---

# Governance Decision 013

Artificial Intelligence supports governance.

AI never replaces governance.

---

# Governance Decision 014

Automation validates governance rules.

Automation never approves architectural changes.

---

# Governance Decision 015

Platform Standards remain mandatory.

Exceptions require explicit approval.

---

# Governance Decision 016

Breaking architectural changes require formal review.

Architecture evolves intentionally.

---

# Governance Decision 017

Every Platform Capability remains independently evolvable.

Evolution remains modular.

---

# Governance Decision 018

Platform Documentation evolves together with the Platform.

Documentation is part of the Product.

---

# Governance Decision 019

Every strategic decision preserves long-term sustainability.

Short-term optimization never compromises the future.

---

# Governance Decision 020

Governance continuously evolves.

Architecture remains timeless.
