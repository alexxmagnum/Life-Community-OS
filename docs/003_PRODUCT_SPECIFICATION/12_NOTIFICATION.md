# 12_NOTIFICATION

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Document Type: Product Specification
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the concept of a Notification within Life Community OS.

A Notification represents a message generated by the platform to inform a Person about something that requires awareness or action.

Notifications support participation.

They should never become noise.

---

# Question this document answers

> What is a Notification?

---

# Scope

This specification defines the product behaviour of Notifications.

It describes:

- information delivery;
- awareness;
- context;
- relationships;
- lifecycle.

It does not define:

- push providers;
- email providers;
- SMS;
- implementation;
- delivery infrastructure.

---

# Definition

A Notification is a contextual communication generated by the platform.

Its purpose is to attract attention to something relevant.

A Notification never exists without a reason.

---

# Examples

Examples include:

- Experience Reminder
- Reservation Confirmation
- Marketplace Interest
- New Community Project
- Mobility Update
- New Conversation Message
- Membership Approval
- Organization Invitation
- System Announcement
- Security Alert

The same Notification model supports all of them.

---

# A Notification Is Not a Conversation

Notifications inform.

Conversations communicate.

A Notification may lead to a Conversation.

The concepts remain independent.

---

# A Notification Is Not an Experience

Experiences generate Notifications.

Notifications do not define Experiences.

---

# A Notification Is Not an Announcement

Announcements are content.

Notifications inform People that relevant content exists.

---

# Responsibilities

A Notification is responsible for:

- informing;
- attracting attention;
- providing context;
- encouraging timely participation.

Nothing more.

---

# Context

Every Notification should refer to a context.

Examples include:

- Experience
- Marketplace Listing
- Mobility Offer
- Community Project
- Conversation
- Entity
- Membership
- Reservation

Context explains why the Notification exists.

---

# Recipient

Notifications are always addressed to one or more recipients.

Recipients may include:

- a Person;
- members of a Community;
- representatives of an Entity;
- administrators.

Delivery depends on recipient preferences.

---

# Priority

Notifications may define priority.

Examples include:

- Informational
- Important
- Urgent
- Critical

Priority should remain meaningful.

It should never be abused.

---

# Delivery

Notifications may be delivered through different channels.

Examples include:

- in-app;
- push;
- email;
- SMS;
- future communication channels.

Delivery channels are implementation details.

The Notification remains the same.

---

# Visibility

Notifications should respect:

- user preferences;
- privacy;
- quiet hours;
- accessibility settings.

Communication should remain respectful.

---

# Relationships

Notifications may relate to:

- People
- Experiences
- Marketplace Listings
- Mobility Offers
- Community Projects
- Conversations
- Entities
- Memberships

---

# Product Rules

Every Notification has a reason.

Every Notification belongs to a context.

Duplicate Notifications should be avoided.

Notifications should encourage action rather than interrupt unnecessarily.

---

# Lifecycle

Typical lifecycle:

Generated

↓

Delivered

↓

Seen

↓

Acknowledged

↓

Archived

Expired Notifications may be removed according to platform policy.

---

# Future Evolution

Future versions may support:

- AI prioritization;
- notification grouping;
- smart scheduling;
- predictive reminders;
- multilingual optimization;
- adaptive delivery.

These additions should preserve the conceptual definition.

---

# Future Implications

This specification directly influences:

- Conversations
- Experiences
- Discovery
- Search
- AI
- Automation
- Analytics
- Accessibility

---

# Success Criteria

The Notification specification is successful when:

- every Notification has a clear purpose;
- users are informed without being overwhelmed;
- delivery remains contextual;
- priorities remain meaningful;
- future delivery technologies reuse the same model.

---

# Conclusion

Notifications connect People with what matters at the right moment.

They exist to improve participation, awareness and coordination without becoming a source of distraction.

Life Community OS values relevance over quantity.

---

*"The best notification is the one that arrives at the right moment with a clear purpose."*
