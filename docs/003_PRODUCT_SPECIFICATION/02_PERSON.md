# 02_PERSON

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Document Type: Product Specification
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the concept of a Person within Life Community OS.

A Person represents a real human being interacting with the platform.

Everything related to participation begins with a Person.

A Person exists independently of any Territory, Membership, Organization or Experience.

---

# Question this document answers

> What is a Person?

---

# Scope

This specification defines the product behaviour of Persons.

It describes:

- what a Person represents;
- its responsibilities;
- its relationships;
- its lifecycle.

It does not define:

- authentication;
- login;
- permissions;
- database schemas;
- technical implementation.

---

# Definition

A Person is a unique human being recognized by the platform.

A Person may:

- live somewhere;
- visit somewhere;
- work somewhere;
- participate somewhere;
- organize activities;
- belong to organizations;
- belong to communities.

These relationships may change.

The Person remains the same.

---

# A Person Is Not a User Account

A user account is one way a Person accesses the platform.

It is not the Person itself.

Authentication belongs to Platform Architecture.

Identity belongs to the Person.

---

# A Person Is Not a Membership

Membership defines belonging.

A Person exists before belonging.

A Person may have:

- no Membership;
- one Membership;
- multiple Memberships.

Membership never defines identity.

---

# A Person Is Not an Organization

Organizations participate through People.

A Person may represent an Organization.

The Organization is a different concept.

---

# Responsibilities

A Person is responsible for:

- participating;
- communicating;
- collaborating;
- creating;
- joining;
- contributing.

Nothing else.

---

# Identity

Every Person has a unique identity.

Identity remains stable regardless of:

- Territory;
- language;
- organization;
- role;
- permissions;
- reputation.

Identity is permanent.

Relationships evolve.

---

# Participation

People may participate in many ways.

Examples include:

- joining Experiences;
- creating Community Projects;
- publishing Marketplace Listings;
- volunteering;
- organizing activities;
- collaborating with organizations;
- sharing mobility;
- helping neighbours.

Participation defines community life.

---

# Representation

A Person may represent:

- themselves;
- an Organization;
- an Association;
- a Business;
- a School;
- another Entity through explicit authorization.

Representation never changes identity.

---

# Privacy

A Person controls the visibility of personal information whenever possible.

The platform should expose only information required for healthy community interaction.

Privacy is a constitutional principle.

---

# Relationships

A Person may relate to:

- Territories;
- Memberships;
- Entities;
- Experiences;
- Community Projects;
- Conversations;
- Marketplace Listings;
- Mobility Offers;
- Media;
- Notifications.

Relationships change continuously.

The Person remains constant.

---

# Product Rules

A Person should never be modeled according to a specific capability.

Marketplace does not create a different Person.

Experiences do not create a different Person.

Administration does not create a different Person.

The same Person participates everywhere.

---

# Future Evolution

Future versions may support:

- multiple identities for privacy-sensitive scenarios;
- delegated representation;
- family relationships;
- emergency contacts;
- accessibility profiles;
- AI personal assistants acting on behalf of the Person with explicit authorization.

These extensions should not modify the conceptual definition of a Person.

---

# Future Implications

This specification directly influences:

- Membership
- Identity
- Trust
- Experiences
- Community Projects
- Marketplace
- Mobility
- Conversations
- Notifications
- Organizations
- Governance

---

# Success Criteria

The Person specification is successful when:

- identity remains stable;
- participation is independent of capabilities;
- relationships evolve without changing identity;
- privacy is respected;
- every capability reuses the same Person concept.

---

# Conclusion

A Person is the central participant of Life Community OS.

Everything else describes where the Person belongs, what the Person does or how the Person participates.

The platform revolves around People.

Never around features.

---

*"Communities are built by People. Software simply helps People connect, collaborate and participate."*
