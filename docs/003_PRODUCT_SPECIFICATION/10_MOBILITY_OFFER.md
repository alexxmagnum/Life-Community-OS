Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the concept of a Mobility Offer within Life Community OS.

A Mobility Offer represents any opportunity for People to share, request or coordinate transportation within or between Territories.

The objective is not transportation itself.

The objective is connecting People through mobility.

---

# Question this document answers

> What is a Mobility Offer?

---

# Scope

This specification defines the product behaviour of Mobility Offers.

It describes:

- transportation opportunities;
- shared mobility;
- travel coordination;
- relationships;
- lifecycle.

It does not define:

- navigation;
- maps;
- GPS;
- payment systems;
- route optimization.

---

# Definition

A Mobility Offer is a published opportunity related to transportation.

It allows People to travel together, request transportation or coordinate journeys.

The platform treats every mobility interaction through the same conceptual model.

---

# Examples

Examples include:

- Offer a Ride
- Need a Ride
- Daily Carpool
- Airport Transfer
- Golf Tournament Shuttle
- School Pickup
- Volunteer Transport
- Grocery Trip
- Beach Excursion
- Bicycle Group Ride
- Walking Group
- Community Bus

The same Mobility model supports all of them.

---

# A Mobility Offer Is Not a Marketplace Listing

Marketplace Listings facilitate exchanges.

Mobility Offers facilitate transportation.

Both connect People.

They solve different problems.

---

# A Mobility Offer Is Not an Experience

An Experience may require transportation.

The Mobility Offer provides access.

The Experience provides purpose.

---

# Responsibilities

A Mobility Offer is responsible for:

- describing transportation;
- connecting travellers;
- defining availability;
- coordinating participation.

Nothing more.

---

# Publisher

A Mobility Offer may be created by:

- a Person;
- an Entity.

The publisher coordinates the mobility opportunity.

---

# Participation

Participants may be:

- driver;
- passenger;
- organizer;
- coordinator;
- volunteer.

Participation remains flexible.

---

# Capacity

A Mobility Offer may define:

- available seats;
- luggage capacity;
- accessibility options;
- pets allowed;
- bicycles allowed.

Capacity belongs to the Offer.

---

# Schedule

Mobility Offers may be:

- one-time;
- recurring;
- flexible;
- on-demand.

Scheduling should remain configurable.

---

# Route

A Mobility Offer may describe:

- origin;
- destination;
- intermediate stops;
- meeting point.

Routes support coordination.

They do not define the Offer.

---

# Trust

Mobility requires trust.

The platform may consider:

- verified Membership;
- profile completeness;
- community history;
- mutual connections.

Trust supports safe participation.

---

# Discovery

Mobility Offers should be discoverable through:

- Territory;
- destination;
- schedule;
- proximity;
- interests;
- recommendations.

---

# Relationships

Mobility Offers may relate to:

- People
- Territories
- Experiences
- Community Projects
- Conversations
- Notifications

Optionally:

- Marketplace Listings

---

# Product Rules

Every Mobility Offer has one publisher.

Participation is voluntary.

Mobility remains independent from navigation providers.

Payments remain optional.

---

# Lifecycle

↓

↓

Open

↓

Full

Completed

Offers may also be Cancelled.

# Future Evolution

Future versions may support:

- route optimization;
- EV charging suggestions;
- AI passenger matching;
- calendar synchronization;
- multimodal transport;
- public transport integration.

These additions should preserve the conceptual definition.

---

# Future Implications

This specification directly influences:

- Discovery
- Search
- Notifications
- Conversations
- AI
- Analytics
- Experiences

---

# Success Criteria

The Mobility Offer specification is successful when:

- every transportation opportunity follows the same model;
- mobility remains independent from mapping technology;
- coordination remains simple;
- trust encourages participation;
- future transportation models require configuration rather than redesign.

---

# Conclusion

Mobility Offers connect People through transportation.

By treating every journey as an opportunity for collaboration rather than simply movement, Life Community OS strengthens participation beyond the destination itself.
