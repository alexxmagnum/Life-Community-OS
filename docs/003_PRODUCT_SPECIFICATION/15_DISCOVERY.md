# 15_DISCOVERY

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Document Type: Product Specification
Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the concept of Discovery within Life Community OS.

Discovery represents the capability of helping People discover relevant opportunities, activities, organizations and connections they were not actively searching for.

The objective is not searching.

The objective is inspiring participation.

---

# Question this document answers

> How does the platform help People discover opportunities?

---

# Scope

This specification defines the product behaviour of Discovery.

It describes:

- recommendations;
- exploration;
- contextual suggestions;
- relationships.

It does not define:

- recommendation algorithms;
- artificial intelligence implementation;
- search engines;
- advertising systems.

---

# Definition

Discovery is the capability of presenting relevant information before the user explicitly asks for it.

Discovery encourages participation through relevance.

It should never feel intrusive.

---

# Discovery Is Not Search

Search begins with an intention.

Discovery creates new opportunities.

Examples:

Search

↓

"I want to find a tennis lesson."

Discovery

↓

"There is a tennis lesson happening tomorrow near you."

Both capabilities complement each other.

---

# Responsibilities

Discovery is responsible for:

- recommending opportunities;
- increasing participation;
- reducing missed opportunities;
- connecting People with relevant content.

Nothing more.

---

# Discoverable Content

Discovery may recommend:

- Experiences
- Community Projects
- Marketplace Listings
- Mobility Offers
- Entities
- Places
- Volunteers
- Organizations
- Community Initiatives

Future concepts should naturally integrate into Discovery.

---

# Context

Discovery should understand context.

Examples include:

- Territory
- Membership
- preferred language
- interests
- availability
- proximity
- participation history
- favorite activities
- accessibility preferences

Context improves relevance.

---

# Relevance

Discovery should prioritize:

- usefulness;
- proximity;
- community relevance;
- freshness;
- diversity;
- participation value.

The objective is quality.

Not quantity.

---

# Personalization

Discovery should gradually improve as the platform learns from participation.

Examples include:

- attended Experiences;
- favorite categories;
- interactions;
- voluntary preferences.

Personalization should remain transparent.

---

# Community Value

Discovery should encourage healthy community growth.

It should highlight:

- new initiatives;
- local businesses;
- volunteers;
- upcoming Experiences;
- community needs;
- collaboration opportunities.

Community benefit takes precedence over commercial promotion.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence may assist Discovery by:

- identifying relevant opportunities;
- summarizing recommendations;
- suggesting activities;
- detecting participation patterns.

Artificial Intelligence assists.

It never replaces user choice.

---

# Relationships

Discovery may relate to:

- People
- Territories
- Experiences
- Community Projects
- Marketplace Listings
- Mobility Offers
- Entities
- Places
- Notifications

---

# Product Rules

Discovery should never manipulate People.

Recommendations should always remain explainable.

Commercial interests should never dominate community value.

Discovery should respect permissions and privacy.

Users should always remain in control.

---

# Lifecycle

Typical lifecycle:

Context Analysis

↓

Opportunity Selection

↓

Recommendation

↓

Interaction

↓

Learning

↓

Continuous Improvement

# Future Evolution

Future versions may support:

- predictive recommendations;
- seasonal suggestions;
- collaborative recommendations;
- AI-generated itineraries;
- cross-community discovery;
- smart onboarding recommendations.

These additions should preserve the conceptual definition.

---

# Future Implications

This specification directly influences:

- Search
- AI
- Notifications
- Experiences
- Marketplace
- Mobility
- Community Projects
- Accessibility
- Analytics

---

# Success Criteria

The Discovery specification is successful when:

- People regularly discover valuable opportunities;
- recommendations remain relevant;
- participation increases naturally;
- users remain in control;
- future recommendation technologies integrate without changing the conceptual model.

---

# Conclusion

Discovery helps People participate more by revealing opportunities they might otherwise never encounter.

Rather than waiting for People to search, Life Community OS proactively connects them with experiences, projects, organizations and initiatives that enrich community life.
