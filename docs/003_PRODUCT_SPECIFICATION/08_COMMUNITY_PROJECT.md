# Purpose

This document defines the concept of a Community Project within Life Community OS.

A Community Project represents a long-term collaborative initiative driven by one or more People or Entities with the objective of improving, creating or supporting something valuable for the community.

Projects coordinate collaboration over time.

Experiences create moments.

Projects create outcomes.

---

# Question this document answers

> What is a Community Project?

---

# Scope

This specification defines the product behaviour of Community Projects.

It describes:

- collaborative initiatives;
- objectives;
- participation;
- relationships;
- lifecycle.

It does not define:

- individual Experiences;
- conversations;
- tasks implementation;
- project management software.

---

# Definition

A Community Project is a structured initiative with a shared objective.

Projects organize collaboration over days, weeks or months.

They exist to achieve a result.

---

# Examples

Examples include:

- Build a Children's Playground
- Organize the Summer Festival
- Beach Cleanup Initiative
- Improve Community Gardens
- Food Donation Campaign
- Neighbourhood Watch Program
- Sports Tournament Organization
- Christmas Decoration Project
- Local Business Promotion Campaign
- Animal Shelter Support
- Community Library Creation
- Charity Marathon

The platform treats every initiative through the same model.

---

# A Community Project Is Not an Experience

Projects pursue objectives.

Experiences are activities.

A Project may generate many Experiences.

Examples:

Community Project

↓

Summer Festival

Experiences

↓

Concert

↓

BBQ

↓

Children's Games

↓

Sports Tournament

---

# A Community Project Is Not an Entity

Entities may participate.

Entities may sponsor.

Entities may organize.

The Project remains independent.

---

# A Community Project Is Not a Conversation

Projects generate conversations.

Conversations support Projects.

They remain different concepts.

---

# Responsibilities

A Community Project is responsible for:

- defining an objective;
- coordinating collaboration;
- connecting participants;
- grouping related Experiences;
- measuring progress;
- creating long-term value.

Nothing more.

---

# Participation

People may participate as:

- creator;
- coordinator;
- volunteer;
- collaborator;
- sponsor;
- participant;
- supporter.

Participation should remain flexible.

---

# Organization

Projects may be initiated by:

- a Person;
- an Entity;
- multiple Entities;
- the Community itself.

Ownership may evolve.

The Project remains.

---

# Progress

Projects evolve over time.

Typical indicators may include:

- objectives;
- milestones;
- participation;
- resources required;
- completion percentage;
- community impact.

Progress belongs to the Project.

---

# Resources

Projects may use:

- Places;
- Resources;
- Experiences;
- Media;
- Volunteers;
- Sponsorship.

None are mandatory.

Projects should remain lightweight.

---

# Collaboration

Projects encourage collaboration between:

- People;
- Entities;
- Organizations;
- Volunteers;
- Sponsors.

Collaboration is the primary purpose.

---

# Community Impact

Projects should leave a lasting positive impact.

Examples include:

- infrastructure;
- wellbeing;
- education;
- culture;
- environment;
- inclusion;
- local economy.

Projects strengthen communities beyond individual events.

---

# Relationships

Community Projects may relate to:

- People
- Entities
- Territories
- Places
- Resources
- Experiences
- Marketplace Listings
- Conversations
- Media
- Notifications

---

# Product Rules

Every Project has one clearly defined objective.

Projects may generate multiple Experiences.

Projects should remain independent from organizations.

Projects should encourage open participation whenever appropriate.

---

# Lifecycle

Typical lifecycle:

Idea

↓

Planning

↓

Active

↓

In Progress

↓

Completed

↓

Archived

Projects may also be Cancelled.

# Future Evolution

Future versions may support:

- funding campaigns;
- public voting;
- participatory budgeting;
- AI-assisted planning;
- milestone automation;
- impact scoring;
- cross-community collaboration.

These extensions should preserve the conceptual definition.

---

# Future Implications

This specification directly influences:

- Experiences
- Marketplace
- Automation
- AI
- Notifications
- Discovery
- Search
- Governance
- Analytics

---

# Success Criteria

The Community Project specification is successful when:

- every long-term initiative fits the same model;
- Projects remain independent from Experiences;
- collaboration remains simple;
- objectives remain clear;
- future collaboration models require configuration rather than redesign.

---

# Conclusion

Community Projects represent the long-term ambitions of a community.

While Experiences create moments, Projects create lasting change.

Life Community OS should encourage communities not only to meet together, but also to build meaningful outcomes together.

---

*"Experiences bring people together. Projects help them build something that remains after the experience ends."*
