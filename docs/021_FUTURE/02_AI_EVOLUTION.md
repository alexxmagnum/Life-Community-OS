# Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution of Artificial Intelligence inside Life Community OS.

Artificial Intelligence should continuously improve while remaining governed by Platform Architecture.

AI evolves rapidly.

Architecture evolves deliberately.

---

# Question this document answers

> How should Artificial Intelligence evolve without compromising Platform Architecture?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- AI evolution;
- AI governance;
- provider independence;
- future AI capabilities;
- architectural compatibility.

It does not define:

- prompt implementation;
- model selection;
- infrastructure;
- commercial strategy.

---

# Definition

Artificial Intelligence is a reusable Platform Capability that augments human decision-making and Platform Behaviour.

AI remains governed.

Business Behaviour remains deterministic.

# Objectives

AI Evolution exists to:

- reduce provider lock-in;

- improve intelligence;
- maintain governance;
