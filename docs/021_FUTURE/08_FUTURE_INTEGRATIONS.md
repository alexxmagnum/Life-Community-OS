Version: 1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term Integration Evolution Strategy of Life Community OS.

Future integrations should become plug-and-play while preserving Platform Architecture, Business Behaviour and Governance.

Providers evolve.

Standards evolve.

Architecture remains stable.

---

# Question this document answers

> How should the Platform integrate with future technologies without architectural redesign?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- future connectors;
- interoperability;
- provider evolution;
- protocol evolution;
- compatibility.

It does not define:

- provider SDKs;
- implementation details;
- infrastructure;
- deployment.

---

# Definition

Future Integrations connect external ecosystems to Life Community OS through governed Platform Connectors.

Business Domains remain provider-independent.

---

# Objectives

Future Integrations exist to:

- maximize interoperability;
- reduce vendor lock-in;
- simplify connector development;
- improve portability;
- preserve architectural consistency;
- support decades of innovation.
