# Scope

This document defines:

- platform intelligence;
- collective learning;
- operational intelligence;
- decision intelligence;
- architectural compatibility.

It does not define:

- AI models;
- autonomous agents;
- provider implementation;
- infrastructure.

# Definition

Platform Intelligence is the collective intelligence emerging from Platform Capabilities, Business Domains, Knowledge, Analytics, Automation and Artificial Intelligence working together.

Platform Intelligence belongs to the Platform.

Not to any individual AI model.

---

# Objectives

Platform Intelligence exists to:

- improve decision quality;
- optimize operations;
- discover patterns;
- support proactive recommendations;
- accumulate organizational knowledge;
- support decades of evolution.
