Version: 1.0
Status: Living

# Purpose

This document defines how Platform Architecture evolves throughout the lifetime of Life Community OS.

Architecture should continuously improve while preserving its core principles.

Evolution is expected.

Architectural integrity is mandatory.

---

# Question this document answers

> How can Platform Architecture evolve without breaking itself?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- architectural evolution;
- compatibility;
- migration strategy;
- future-proofing;
- governance.

It does not define:

- implementation details;
- technologies;
- roadmap priorities;
- business planning.

---

# Definition

Architecture Evolution is the controlled improvement of Platform Architecture while preserving compatibility and long-term sustainability.

Evolution never compromises architectural foundations.

---

# Objectives

Architecture Evolution exists to:

- preserve architectural integrity;
- simplify future innovation;
- reduce technical debt;
- improve maintainability;
- maximize compatibility;
- support decades of growth.

---

# Evolution Philosophy

Architecture evolves through refinement.

It does not evolve through replacement.

Foundations remain stable.

Implementations evolve.

---

# Architectural Layers

The following layers should remain stable:

Principles

↓

Capabilities

↓

Domains

↓

Contracts

↓

Security

↓

Governance

↓

Documentation

Only implementation layers evolve.

---

# Evolution Rules

Every architectural evolution should:

respect Architectural Principles;

preserve Platform Capabilities;

maintain Business Domains;

avoid duplicated responsibilities;

remain technology independent;

improve maintainability.

# Compatibility

Backward Compatibility

Forward Compatibility

Migration Complexity

Operational Impact

Business Impact

Knowledge Impact

Compatibility remains measurable.

# Architectural Refactoring

reduce complexity;

preserve behaviour.
