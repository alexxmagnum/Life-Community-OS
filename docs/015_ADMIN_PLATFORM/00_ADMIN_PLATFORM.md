# Purpose

This document defines the Administrative Platform Architecture of Life Community OS.

The Administrative Platform provides every administrative experience required to operate, manage, monitor and evolve the platform while preserving Business Behaviour, Security and User Experience.

The Administrative Platform belongs to the Core Platform.

Business Domains expose administrative capabilities through it.

---

# Question this document answers

> What is the Administrative Platform inside Life Community OS?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- administrative platform architecture;
- administrative philosophy;
- administrative responsibilities;
- administrative surfaces;
- long-term evolution.

It does not define:

- visual design;
- UI implementation;
- frameworks;
- infrastructure.

---

# Definition

The Administrative Platform is the unified environment where authorized users operate, configure, supervise and manage Life Community OS.

It provides administration.

It never owns Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

The Administrative Platform exists to:

- centralize administration;
- improve operational efficiency;
- simplify business management;
- increase productivity;
- support multiple administrative roles;
- enable long-term scalability.

---

# Administrative Philosophy

Business Behaviour belongs to Business Domains.

Administration belongs to the Administrative Platform.

Every administrative experience should remain consistent.
