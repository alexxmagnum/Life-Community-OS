Version: 1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the Administrative Workspace Architecture of Life Community OS.

Administrative Workspaces provide focused operational environments where administrative users execute Business Operations efficiently while preserving consistency, productivity and architectural simplicity.

Administrative Workspaces belong to the Administrative Platform.

Business Domains remain workspace-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How are Business Operations organized into productive administrative workspaces?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- workspace architecture;
- workspace composition;
- workspace lifecycle;
- contextual operation;
- governance.

It does not define:

- visual layouts;
- UI components;
- implementation details;
- infrastructure.

---

# Definition

Administrative Workspaces are focused operational environments composed of reusable Administrative Capabilities.

Workspaces organize work.

Business Domains provide Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

Administrative Workspaces exist to:

- reduce context switching;
- improve productivity;
- simplify Business Operations;
- maximize capability reuse;
- support multiple administrative roles;
- enable long-term scalability.
