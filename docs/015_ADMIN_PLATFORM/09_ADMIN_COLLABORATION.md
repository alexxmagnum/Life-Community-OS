# Purpose

This document defines the Administrative Collaboration Architecture of Life Community OS.

Administrative Collaboration enables multiple administrative users to coordinate, communicate and execute Business Operations together while preserving Security, Accountability and Business Integrity.

Administrative Collaboration belongs to the Administrative Platform.

Business Domains remain collaboration-independent.

---

# Question this document answers

> How do administrative users collaborate inside Life Community OS?

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- collaboration architecture;
- collaborative workflows;
- shared operational context;
- accountability;
- governance.

It does not define:

- chat applications;
- video conferencing;
- messaging providers;
- implementation details.

---

# Definition

Administrative Collaboration provides shared operational capabilities that improve coordination between administrative users.

Collaboration improves execution.

It never changes Business Behaviour.

---

# Objectives

Administrative Collaboration exists to:

- improve team coordination;
- reduce duplicated work;
- simplify communication;
- increase accountability;
- improve operational visibility;
- support long-term scalability.
